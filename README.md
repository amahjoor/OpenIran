# OpenIran

## About 
This project was created to consolidate information about what's happening in Iran as the war has escalated. The information that comes out of the war is often fragmented, the internet is frequently blacked out for people within the country, and news coverage on the war is often biased or unclear. 

There are tell-tale signals that can give us insight into what's happening within the country and in terms of the war. We capture 1) aviation data to see airplanes flying into the airport or over the country, 2) internet outages, 3) trends in strikes, and 4) news coverage. 

My name is Arman, and I am building and maintaining OpenIran as I have family in Iran and wanted to build one central source of truth into what's happening there. This project is open source and anyone can use it or build upon it. All contributions are welcomed, and donations are also appreciated. I hope this tool is useful for anyone else who wants to stay informed about the situation in Iran.
